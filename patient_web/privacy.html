Niramay Privacy Policy

Niramay helps patients find participating hospitals, doctors, and clinics, view appointment availability, and request visit bookings online.

Google user data

Google sign-in is used to identify the patient account by email and keep booking access tied to the signed-in user. When a patient allows Google Drive access, booking receipts may be saved to the patient's private Google Drive app data folder so booking history can be restored across devices.

Patient and booking data

Patient name, phone, and local receipts are stored in the browser using encrypted local storage. Appointment details needed for a booking may be shared with the selected participating hospital, clinic, or doctor.

Service limits

This service is for appointment discovery and booking requests only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, emergency support, or guaranteed appointment acceptance.
